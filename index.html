Features

  • Fill web forms with realistic, edge-case or deliberately invalid test data
  • Handles multi-step forms, file uploads and date pickers
  • Validation reports to see how a form reacts to the data
  • Keyboard shortcut: Alt+Shift+F fills all fields on the page
  • Right-click menu for quick access

Support

Found a bug, have a question or want to suggest a feature? Please open an issue on GitHub.

Please include your Chrome version, the FillPilot version and the page where the problem happened.

FAQ

A field wasn't filled. Some custom widgets load late. Wait for the page to finish loading and run FillPilot again.

The shortcut doesn't work. Check or change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

FillPilot does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

  • No data leaves your device. FillPilot makes no network requests and has no servers, analytics or tracking.
  • Local storage only. Your settings and profiles are saved in your browser with Chrome's local extension storage. They stay on your computer, and you can delete them by removing the extension.
  • Page access. FillPilot needs access to web pages only so it can find and fill form fields when you ask it to. Page content is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
