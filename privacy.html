FillPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how the FillPilot browser extension ("FillPilot", "we") handles information. In short: FillPilot does not collect, transmit, sell or share any user data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data. FillPilot has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it makes no network requests.

2. Data stored on your device

To work, FillPilot saves the following in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • Your settings and custom fill rules
  • Test-data profiles and generated test personas (fictional data)
  • Test files you choose to save for filling file-upload fields
  • The most recent form validation report

This data never leaves your computer and is not accessible to us. You can delete it at any time by removing the extension from Chrome.

3. Website access

FillPilot needs permission to access web pages so it can find and fill form fields when you ask it to. It reads page content only to detect form fields. It does not record, store or transmit the content of the pages you visit, your browsing history, or anything you type.

4. Sharing of data

We do not sell, transfer or share user data with any third party. We do not use data for any purpose unrelated to FillPilot's single purpose, and we do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

5. Children's privacy

FillPilot is a developer and QA testing tool. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub
